Metal fins and contact surfaces have sharper reflections than the nonmetallic polymer. PCB, chip bodies, ceramic passives, solder, gold contacts and thermal pads retain distinct colors and roughness. The dark rear plate and blade cavities retain visible detail. The primary hero keeps parallel mating surfaces and exposes all major layers; cooler contact faces are shown separately from below.

Defects corrected: the cyan diffuser was inside the solid shroud skirt and only illuminated nearby metal. Added a real viewing channel and positioned the diffuser flush with the outside edge. Several detail-camera rolls left labels upside down; rolled the PCB, fan-back and rear cameras without mirroring geometry. The same corrected model was re-rendered at final resolution.

Gate: PASS for technical presentation. Actual primary output is 3000 × 3200, 96 Cycles samples; macros/front/rear heroes are 2000 × 1400, 64 samples. Neutral and beauty presets are separate. Fine fin edges can alias at reduced display sizes. Surface finishes are intentionally clean and somewhat stylized; connector mouths, cosmetic traces and repeated component types are not manufacturing drawings.
