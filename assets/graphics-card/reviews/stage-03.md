Inspected corrected images: `stage-03-exploded.png`, `stage-03-assembled.png`, `stage-03-side.png`, `stage-03-Fan_front.png`, `stage-03-Fan_back.png`, `stage-03-Shroud_under.png`, `stage-03-Mount.png`.

Three complete fan units each have nine blades, a shaft-centered rotor, motor housing, four rear supports, ears, cable and plug. The shell underside has real circular openings, ribs, skirts and bosses. Mount detail exposes a recessed hex head, shaft, washer and backplate opening. Fins remain exclusively in the cooling module.

Visible defects corrected: initial blade sweep was too shallow; increased planform curvature and pitch. Ring rims were faceted; increased resolution and smoothed cylindrical faces. Fan macros cropped the frame and cable; widened both cameras. The exploded camera cropped distant fasteners; increased its scale from 0.53 to 0.58 m, and side camera to 0.53 m. The first mounting camera was obscured by fins; replaced it with an underside view during initial fastener withdrawal. Increased shell aperture cut resolution.

Gate: PASS. Blade roots intentionally enter their hubs; fan blades otherwise have positive housing and support clearance. Detailed thread helices and electrical connector latches are simplified. Small aperture bevel highlights remain visible at extreme magnification, without silhouette gaps.
